# 12. Feedback control is Modified: the instruments exist and nothing routes a finding

Date: 2026-09-15

## Status

Proposed

## Context

The whiteboard's feedback control system routes signals from every machine to operators, maintainers
and execs, with two audiences: the exec team through advisor-builder and the operators through the
learning-curve method. Measured 2026-09-09 in design-loop: the instruments exist and are unjoined —
operator-moves, ask-yield, batch-rca, the failure ledger, three Brier surfaces and a claim ledger —
and nothing decides who a finding is for. The calibration engine is a requirement of this container
(CAL1) and not built: the figure called a Brier score is the average confidence on known-false claims.

## Decision

Feedback control is drawn as one Modified container: routes a finding, and scores whether the agent
knew. Modified because the instruments are the seat and they exist; the routing and the engine are
the gaps the box names.

## Consequences

The learning-curve half, once the L&D department in ADR 1, is a description inside this box until
its return is drawn.
