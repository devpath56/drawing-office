# 6. Distribution is a Proposal, owned by the Sales department

Date: 2026-09-15

## Status

Proposed

## Context

The whiteboard's distribution machine is a smart funnel: hackathons, coffee chats, interviews,
inbound marketing, direct messages. Measured 2026-09-09 in design-loop: nothing — no stage names a
funnel or an audience. design-loop PD-033 made Sales the fourth department of the factory, with the
SMAAT funnel as its store and the blog line as its first product; PD-042 added distribution as roster
job 15.

## Decision

Distribution is drawn as one Proposal container: finished material reaches an audience that answers
back. The audience is an external system the container talks to, and the return edge into feedback
control is drawn with a description that begins with the fact that nothing exists to cross it yet.

## Consequences

The picture claims a funnel it does not have and says so on the box. The tag is retired when a
distribution stage lands, with its own record citing PD-033.
