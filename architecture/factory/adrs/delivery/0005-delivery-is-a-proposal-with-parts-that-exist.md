# 5. Delivery is a Proposal, although its gates already exist

Date: 2026-09-15

## Status

Proposed

## Context

The whiteboard's delivery machine executes agreed work, manages risk and tracks the top metrics.
Measured 2026-09-09 in design-loop: there is no pipe. The land gate (`checks/land.mjs`), the falsifier
and the discipline check exist, and the pipe model's own header says guards are not stages. CL-004
(0.85, OPEN) records that nothing refuses a commit for naming no admitted requirement. The operator
wants practice sources found before the framework is chosen.

## Decision

Delivery is drawn as one Proposal container: an admitted job becomes landed, proven work. The land
door that the handoff trace crosses is described inside it as the part that exists, so the box does
not claim a pipe it lacks and does not hide the gate it has.

## Consequences

The handoff trace's land hop points at a Proposal box on purpose: the reader sees that the day's work
lands through a door that has no pipe behind it. The Proposal tag is retired when a delivery pipe is
ruled and drawn, with its own record.
